# Validation run — 2026-11-11 (Gold MGC + Nasdaq MNQ, NY & Asia)

Data: Yahoo 5m, 2026-08-21 to 2026-10-30 (50 session days). Setup: 1 contract, 1 tick slip + $2.50/side, stop = 1 ATR of execution bar, flat at session end (NY 16:00 ET, Asia 03:00 ET), test over at first -$1,000 trailing closed-trade DD. 13 families, 300 configs per market+session. Results: results/validation_2026-11-11.

## Tests (pass rule)
Initial: net>0, never -$1,000, >=20 trades. Walk-forward: anchored 5 folds; OOS net>0. PBO (CSCV, 8 blocks): <0.5. Deflated Sharpe (N=300): >=0.95. Cost stress: 1.5 tick, 2 tick, $3.75 comm, 2 tick+$3.75 all positive and not busted. Nearby: >=60% of one-step neighbours profitable.

## Findings
- No strategy passes all 6; deflated Sharpe fails everywhere (50 days, 300 trials).
- 5/6: MNQ Asia FVG 5/15/30 alignment (fvg_vwap, 2.5R); MNQ Asia FVG+order block 15m (2R); MNQ NY FVG+order block 30m (2.5R, 21 trades).
- Gold best: MGC Asia Lima BB+RSI (bb 2, rsi 25, 2R) 4/6; MGC NY Oscar 15m ORB retest on 10m bars 3R 3/6 (+$1,189).
- The engine's built-in DD stop skipped trades that would breach, parking busted strategies at ~$998 DD; the strict rule is used instead.
